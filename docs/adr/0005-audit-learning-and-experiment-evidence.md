# ADR-0005: Separate learning evidence from exploration

## Status

Accepted for issue #18.

## Context

The September 2026 audit found compressed examples, disconnected theory tabs,
and canonical visual models that did not demonstrate the task being described.
Opening a lesson supplied no evidence of understanding.

## Decision

Keep existing source records and citation identities. Add structured original
lesson guides with prerequisites, named reasoning status, explanatory sections,
and transfer exercises with staged feedback. Show theory and applications
continuously in Learn mode and retain a compact Revise mode. Store only explicit
self-assessment and a resumption point locally on the device.

Use exact visual models for the principal audited mismatches and clearly label
sampled results and comparison models. A missing sampled implicit surface does
not assert an empty mathematical zero set. Curve continuity checks remain
heuristic even with adaptive subdivision and clipping in all coordinates.

## Consequences

The site can support study and independent checks without permissioned PDFs.
Self-assessment is not an automatic grade, and the audit backlog is not an
assertion that all bespoke visual proposals are implemented. Existing model
and source references remain available for revision and comparison.
