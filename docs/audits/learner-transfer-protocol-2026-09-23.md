# Formative learner transfer protocol

Status: prepared; no learner sessions observed. No learning gain is claimed.

Recruit 3–5 intended pure-mathematics learners who have not seen the tasks below. Record prior calculus exposure without collecting names. Give each learner an unfamiliar task before the lesson, allow the relevant learning path, then give the paired task without notes or graph controls. Ask for spoken or written reasoning, not a multiple-choice guess. Rotate task order across learners. Do not show a worked solution before the paired task.

| Path | Before lesson | After lesson | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One-variable foundations | Find the domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{3-x}/(x+1)$ and decide whether the limit as $x\to-1$ is a function value. | Find the domain of $g(x)=\log(4-x)/(x-2)$; decide whether a value assigned at $x=2$ could change its punctured limit. | States domain restrictions separately; distinguishes nearby behavior from point value. |
| Mean values and approximation | Decide whether Rolle applies to $|x|$ on $[-1,1]$ and name the failing assumption. | Decide whether the mean value theorem applies to $|x-1|$ on $[0,2]$ and explain why its average slope gives no required interior derivative. | Checks continuity and interior differentiability before using a theorem. |
| Several-variable differentiability | For $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ at the origin, propose a linear map and test its normalized remainder. | For $g(x,y)=xy/\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ off the origin, with $g(0,0)=0$, determine the candidate map from coordinate partials and test the diagonal remainder. | Uses a two-dimensional estimate or explicit obstruction; does not infer total differentiability from partials alone. |
| Bounds and orientation | Write polar bounds for $x^2+y^2\le 2x$ and explain the ray endpoint. | Write polar bounds for $x^2+y^2\le 4y$; then state how reversing a closed curve's orientation changes a scalar line integral versus circulation. | Derives the radial bound from the region; distinguishes unsigned measure from oriented vector integral. |

For each task, record the objects/domain named, theorem hypotheses checked, decisive calculation, justification of the conclusion, whether a hint was needed, and the exact wrong step when reasoning failed. Score each dimension 0 (missing or incorrect), 1 (partly justified), or 2 (independent and correct). Record use of time, visits, and sliders separately; none is a mastery score. After each session, identify the lesson block tied to an observed error and revise that block before repeating the task with a new learner. Do not reuse a learner's post-task as an independent retest.
