# Why the Proof workshop is a separate workspace

Issue [247](https://github.com/Jerome-Group/calculus/issues/247) asks learners to construct an object, inspect an exact result, and revise their reasoning. Existing comparison labs expose authored analytic models. Clinic accepts choices among authored alternatives. The workshop adds original bounded constructions whose submitted values decide an exact certificate, obstruction, or restricted conclusion.

Two complete design packages were compared before production implementation. The standalone candidate A keeps construction, result and revision together across three techniques. Embedded candidate B distributes these actions between lesson modes and reduces shell changes. The independent comparison scored A 21 and B 20 out of 25 across mathematical soundness, construction value, coherence, maintainability and integration. These scores express design judgment, not verified learner benefit.

The chosen design uses A's standalone workspace and three mathematical families. It adopts B's closed drafting/inspected phases, explicit attempt identity, immutable pairing of construction with initial explanation and result, and later appended revisions. It also adopts direct lesson entries and text equivalents for formulas. A's request-ID protocol was rejected because checks are synchronous and local. Repeating a check while inspected returns the same state. A broad evaluator or plugin engine would add expression syntax and proof-rule ownership without helping these three constructions.

The prototypes are retained as ignored design evidence in their isolated worktrees and release evidence. They are throwaway sketches, never production checker code. The standalone prototype's mismatched first explanation and latest result were rejected. The production attempt owns all four paired values. Editing starts a new draft, clears the current visible result, preserves the previous attempt in inspectable history, and starts a blank explanation. Supplied starting values are labeled explicitly.

## Exact mathematical contracts

For the first investigation, fix $f(x,y)=2x-y+3x^2+y^2$ on the real plane at the origin, with Euclidean norm $r$. The learner proposes $L=px+qy$ and a positive rational $C$ for $|f-L|\le Cr^2$. If $p\ne2$, let $a=2-p$ and take $(\operatorname{sign}(a)/j,0)$. The normalized remainder is $|a|+3/j$, with nonzero limit. If $p=2$ but $q\ne-1$, use $a=-1-q$ and $(0,\operatorname{sign}(a)/j)$; the quotient is $|a|+1/j$. These sequences refute the proposed derivative. They do not refute differentiability of the function or assert failure of every finite epsilon test.

For the correct map, $R=3x^2+y^2\le3r^2$. Every $C\ge3$ gives a universal certificate. For every positive real $\varepsilon$, choose $\delta=\varepsilon/C$. Then $0<r<\delta$ gives $|R|/r\le Cr<\varepsilon$, including the sharp equality $C=3$. An entered rational epsilon merely illustrates this rule. If $0<C<3$, the sequence $(1/j,0)$ violates the claimed bound at every point since $R/r^2=3$. This refutes that coefficient, not the derivative.

For the second investigation, fix $g=x^3y/(x^6+y^2)$ away from zero and $g(0)=0$. The learner constructs $(t,kt^n)$ with $t>0$, rational $k$ and integer $1\le n\le6$. When $k=0$, the restriction is zero. When $k\ne0,n<3$, cancel powers to get $kt^{3-n}/(t^{6-2n}+k^2)\to0$. For $n>3$, use $kt^{n-3}/(1+k^2t^{2n-6})\to0$. These restricted results leave the full limit unresolved by the submitted path.

When $n=3,k\ne0$, the restriction is the exact nonzero constant $c=k/(1+k^2)$, while the x-axis gives zero. Set $\varepsilon_0=|c|/2$. For arbitrary $\delta>0$, choose $t=\min(1,\delta/[2(1+|k|)])$. Then $0<\|\gamma(t)\|\le t+|k|t^3\le(1+|k|)t\le\delta/2<\delta$, and $|g(\gamma(t))|=|c|>\varepsilon_0$. This includes the branch $t=1$ and arbitrarily small nonzero rational coefficients. Two different path limits rule out every full-limit value.

For the final investigation, fix $F=(-2y/(x^2+y^2),2x/(x^2+y^2))$ on the punctured plane. Three through eight ordered rational vertices define an implicitly closed polygon. A segment from $a$ to $b$ contains zero exactly when $a\times b=0$ and $a\cdot b\le0$. Origin vertices, repeated adjacent vertices and such singular segments are refused. Self-intersections away from zero are allowed.

The winding count adds one when $a_y\le0<b_y$ and $a\times b>0$, and subtracts one when $b_y\le0<a_y$ and $a\times b<0$. The ray intersection has $x=(a\times b)/(b_y-a_y)$. Half-open conditions count vertices once; tangencies cancel and collinear ray edges leave neighboring edges to determine passage. Along the curve, local smooth angle branches satisfy $d\theta=(-y\,dx+x\,dy)/(x^2+y^2)$. Continue these branches with agreement on overlaps. Closure gives total angle change $2\pi w$, so circulation is $4\pi w$. This argument permits self-intersections and repeated traversal. A nonzero value contradicts the closed-path integral of a global gradient. Zero certifies this loop alone and proves no global potential claim. No use of Green's theorem crosses the missing origin.

## Boundaries and verification

The parser accepts bounded integer, finite-decimal and fraction tokens, normalizes BigInt fractions, and makes every mathematical decision using exact signs and comparisons. No symbolic expression parser, CAS, network service, provider credential, numerical proof threshold, or account writer participates. Numerical SVG drawings illustrate only. Traversal arrows, vertex numbers, exact ordered coordinates and prose equivalents remain available when a projection visually overlaps zero.

One root session map survives workspace and lesson navigation within the tab. Only the selected investigation enters the URL. History contains a validated selection, fixed focus token and bounded scroll position. Constructions and prose never enter URLs, browser storage or account records. Reload and new tabs reset them. Existing 125 lesson IDs, course source links, Clinic, Graph studio and comparison labs remain separate owners.

Focused tests independently substitute the signed witnesses, cover sharp and strict bounds and tiny rationals, check every path exponent, and use manually derived winding fixtures for reversal, double traversal, retracing, self-intersections, ray vertices, ray edges, tangencies and singular segments. The maintained real-browser journey covers construction, refusal, exact scopes, paired history, revision, detours, navigation, keyboard, 320px, CSS zoom, reduced motion and reload. Root owns exact-head independent review and release verification. Agent checks establish no learner mastery or participant usability; learner participants are unavailable. Native sign-in and private course-file access remain outside this feature's evidence.
